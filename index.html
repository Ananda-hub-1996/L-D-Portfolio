Fresh
Eyes

What if onboarding worked both ways?

Conceptualised — not implemented
The idea

Turning new-joiner experience into organisational learning. New hires don't arrive empty-handed — they carry practices, comparisons and outside perspective that quietly disappears once they settle in.

The fresh perspective window

Join Perspective is available Normalise

The window narrows the longer it stays unused.

The proposed journey

ObserveConverseCaptureCurateConnectExplore

Built as informal conversation, not a survey or formal assessment.

Opportunity
A temporary outside view of the organisation, rarely captured before it fades.
Design
A conversational journey, not a survey or formal assessment.
Considerations
Timing within the onboarding window, psychological safety, and a clear owner for what is captured.
Success measures
To be defined at the point of implementation.
The shift

From — new joiner as learner

To — new joiner as observer, and potential source of insight

The insight

New joiners aren't only recipients of organisational knowledge. They can also be temporary observers of it.

03
Selected work

AI
Academy

AI adoption isn't a one-course journey.

The idea

The organisation had an interest in building AI capability. Discovery explored how employees were actually approaching AI, and what influenced adoption — not a problem to fix, but a capability to build.

Business request↓ AI adoption survey↓ Understand current state↓ Identify barriers↓ Design capability journey

Adoption was uneven — some employees weren't fully integrating AI into their work because they didn't fully rely on or trust it.

The model

The AI capability journey

AwarenessAI Explorer
SkillAI Whisperer
ApplicationAI Collaborator
ResponsibilityAI Guardian

Awareness → Skill → Application → Responsibility

Conscious AI adoption

Neither blind dependence nor avoidance.

The goal is AI judgement: know when to use it, know how to work with it, know where it adds value, know where human judgement stays in control.

The shift

From — one AI learning request

To — a progressive capability journey

The insight

Capability can be built progressively, from awareness to responsible application.

Learning
Ecosystem

When an LMS problem wasn't an LMS problem.

The idea

An LMS access complaint traced back to a dependency, not a bug.

The dependency chain

HRMS ↓ Employee dataIncomplete ↓ Integration ↓ LMS / LXP ↓ Learning access

Incomplete HRMS data broke the chain long before it reached the LMS.

Complaint↓ Investigation↓ Root cause↓ Intervention↓ Ongoing control

Required HRMS information wasn't consistently completed. The response coordinated with ZHRs to close the gap.

Manual reconciliation / control loop
HRMS new joiner data↓ Compare with LMS user data↓ Identify missing users↓ Flag to ZHR↓ Check required HRMS fields↓ Resolve↓ Verify integration ↺ starts again
The shift

From — "LMS problem"

To — upstream data / process issue

The insight

Sometimes the root cause of a learning-system issue sits upstream of the learning system itself.
